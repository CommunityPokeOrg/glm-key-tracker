Requirements

  • Model pinned to flash. No hopping to bigger models — flash is the whole point of this campaign.
  • Hard budget cap. When the cap is hit, the key goes quiet. Amount TBD in data.json.
  • Campaign-window-only use. Requests only inside the active window above — outside it, the key stays locked.
  • Existing projects only. sims4-funeral-mod, harmonyos-web3-wallet, and the frameeyeosc fork.
  • No new repos. The key does not fund brand-new projects.

Credits — the three mod-run gates

  1. Something actually gets used day to day — a real thing in real rotation, not a demo.
  2. He can explain the choices made: the language, the libraries, and why this platform.
  3. He can trace what happens end to end when a request is sent — with no AI help during the oral check.

Locking and unlocking is handled by cron on vwh's own server, with a backup lock. This site is a read-only display of hand-edited placeholder data — it does not execute lock/unlock, and it does not manage, accept, or store credentials of any kind.
